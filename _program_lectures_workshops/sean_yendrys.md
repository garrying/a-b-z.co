Sean Yendrys is an independent designer who works in the cultural field, often collaborating with artists, architects, curators and institutions on a wide range of work. He designed the covers for a two-book set exploring infrastructure for the [Princeton Architectural Press](http://www.papress.com/html/our.home.page.tpl){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}, and the signage for the Álvaro Siza exhibition for the [CCA](http://www.cca.qc.ca/en/){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}. The recipient of a MFA from [Yale University School of Art](http://art.yale.edu/){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} in 2014, he also teaches typography at [Parsons The New School](http://www.newschool.edu/parsons/){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}.
