Hong Kong-born Canadian designer Tracy Ma was until recently a driving force behind the irreverent design at *[Bloomberg Businessweek](http://www.bloomberg.com/businessweek){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}*. First working with the magazine in 2011, she was promoted to deputy creative director when [Richard Turley](https://twitter.com/Mr_Turley){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} departed for MTV in 2014. Overseeing the weekly print magazine, she also lead the art direction for its annual Design Conference. Just a few weeks, ago Tracy [announced](https://medium.com/@tracy.ma/after-four-years-i-am-leaving-bloomberg-businessweek-to-join-matter-studios-as-creative-director-294c2e1cfc2e){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} she was leaving to become the Creative Director of Matter Studios, a company emerging from [Medium](https://medium.com/){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}.
