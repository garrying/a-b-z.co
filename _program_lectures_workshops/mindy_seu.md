Mindy is a designer working between Cambridge and New York City. She recently left [2x4](http://2x4.org){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}’s Interactive Media team. Previously she held workshops at the Internet Archive, Berkeley Art Museum, and launched online archives for [_Avant Garde_](http://avantgarde.110west40th.com){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} & [_Eros Magazine_](http://eros.110west40th.com){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}. A graduate of [UCLA’s Design Media Arts](http://dma.ucla.edu){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} program, this fall she will begin studies at [Harvard GSD](http://www.gsd.harvard.edu){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}.
