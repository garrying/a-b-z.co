Mindy is a designer working between Cambridge and New York City. She recently left [2x4](http://2x4.org){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} after 3.5 years on the Interactive Media team. Previously she taught [Interactive 2](http://spring2017.designforthe.net){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} at California College of the Arts, and has held workshops at the Internet Archive, Berkeley Art Museum, and [more](http://www.designforthe.net){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}. She recently launched online archives for [_Avant Garde_](http://avantgarde.110west40th.com){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} & [_Eros Magazine_](http://eros.110west40th.com){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}. A graduate of [UCLA’s Design Media Arts](http://dma.ucla.edu){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} program, she will start as a student at the [Harvard Graduate School of Design](http://www.gsd.harvard.edu){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} in Fall 2017.
