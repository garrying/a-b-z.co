Lauren Wickware likes books. A lot. As a designer, she creates art books whose typography is inextricably linked to the texts and images she juggles between pages, folds and foil stamps. A graduate of [Parsons The New School](http://www.newschool.edu/parsons/), she works with cultural movers and shakers like the [Art Gallery of Hamilton](http://www.artgalleryofhamilton.com/) and the [Aga Khan Museum](https://www.agakhanmuseum.org/). For [Art Gallery of Ontario](https://www.ago.net/), she recently designed the catalogue for Outsiders, a provocative exhibition of American photography from the 20th century.
