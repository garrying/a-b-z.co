Michèle is a designer in Montréal. Her roaming studio has worked with [Droog](http://www.droog.com), [Metahaven](http://sprawl.space), [Mediamatic](https://www.mediamatic.net), [Penguin Random House](http://www.penguinrandomhouse.ca), and [Strelka Institute](http://www.strelka.com/en). She works with open content and open source dynamics in publication design—from sourcing internet comments for print magazines to designing social networks with open source code. Publication as a ‘two-way’ conversation is fundamental in the studio.
