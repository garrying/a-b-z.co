As a designer, Michèle has leapt from Toronto to Amsterdam and Montréal. For cultural research and publication design, she has worked with [Droog](http://www.droog.com), [Metahaven](http://sprawl.space), [Mediamatic](https://www.mediamatic.net), [Penguin Random House](http://www.penguinrandomhouse.ca), [Strelka Institute](http://www.strelka.com/en), and [VPRO](https://www.vpro.nl). She works with open content dynamics—from sourcing internet comments for print magazines to designing social networks with open source code. Michèle is a resident at [l’École de design at UQÀM](https://www.instagram.com/design_uqam/).
