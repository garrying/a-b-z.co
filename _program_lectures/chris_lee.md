Chris is a designer in Toronto and Buffalo. A graduate of [OCAD University](http://www.ocadu.ca) and the [Sandberg Instituut](http://sandberg.nl), he has worked for [_The Walrus_](https://thewalrus.ca/category/issues/) magazine, [Metahaven](https://twitter.com/mthvn), and [Bruce Mau Design](http://www.brucemaudesign.com). Chris explores the relationship between currency and graphic design, particularly typography, as a reflection on design’s entanglement with power. His work speculates on alternative and decolonial forms for exchange. Chris is an Assistant Professor at the [University at Buffalo](http://www.buffalo.edu).
