Xavier is a Toronto-based algorithm designer, entrepreneur, educator and artist. As cofounder and CTO at Whirlscape, he designed the neural networks which predict emoji, stickers, and GIFs in the [Dango](http://getdango.com) app as well as the autocorrect powering the [Minuum](http://minuum.com) keyboard. He organizes the annual [GenArtHackParty](http://genarthackparty.com), that brings both beginners and experienced artists together to learn from each other and build generative artworks.
