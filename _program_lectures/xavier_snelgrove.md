Xavier is a Toronto-based algorithm designer, entrepreneur, educator and artist. As cofounder and CTO at [Whirlscape](http://www.whirlscape.com/){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}, he designed the neural networks which predict emoji, stickers, and GIFs in the [Dango](http://getdango.com){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} app as well as the autocorrect powering the [Minuum](http://minuum.com){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} keyboard. He organizes the annual [GenArtHackParty](http://genarthackparty.com){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}, that brings both beginners and experienced artists together to learn from each other and build generative artworks.
