Chris Lee is a designer and educator based Buﬀalo, NY, and Toronto, ON. He is a graduate of [OCADU](http://www.ocadu.ca){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} and the [Sandberg Instituut](http://sandberg.nl){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}, he has worked for [_The Walrus_](https://thewalrus.ca/category/issues/){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} magazine, _[Scapegoat](http://www.scapegoatjournal.org){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}_ journal, [Metahaven](https://twitter.com/mthvn){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}, and [Bruce Mau Design](http://www.brucemaudesign.com/){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}. Chris’ research explores graphic design’s entanglement with power, standards, and legitimacy. He is an Assistant Professor at the [University at Buffalo](http://www.buffalo.edu){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} SUNY and a research fellow at [Het Nieuwe Instituut](https://hetnieuweinstituut.nl){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}.
