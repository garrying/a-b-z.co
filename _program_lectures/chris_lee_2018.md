Chris is a designer in Toronto and Buffalo. A graduate of [OCAD University](http://www.ocadu.ca){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} and the [Sandberg Instituut](http://sandberg.nl){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}, he has worked for [_The Walrus_](https://thewalrus.ca/category/issues/){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} magazine, [Metahaven](https://twitter.com/mthvn){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}, and [Bruce Mau Design](http://www.brucemaudesign.com){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}. Chris explores the relationship between currency and graphic designers a reflection on design’s entanglement with power. His work speculates on alternative and decolonial forms for exchange. Chris is an Assistant Professor at the [University at Buffalo](http://www.buffalo.edu){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}.
