Nasrin Himada is a Palestinian writer, editor, and curator based in Tio’tia:ke (Montréal), in Kanien’kehá:ka territory. Their writing on contemporary art has appeared in _[Canadian Art](https://canadianart.ca){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}_, _[C Magazine](http://cmagazine.com){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}_, _Critical Signals_, _[The Funambulist](https://thefunambulist.net){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}_, _Fuse Magazine_, and _[MICE Magazine](http://micemagazine.ca){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}_, among others. They are the co-editor of [contemptorary.org](http://contemptorary.org/){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}.
