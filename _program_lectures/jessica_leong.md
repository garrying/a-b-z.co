Jessica is a Toronto-based designer and is currently a senior designer at [Frontier](http://www.frontier.is/). With experience in everything from designing magazines to displays and super graphics, she has previously spent time with E.R.A. Architects, Bruce Mau Design, Indigo and Design Exchange. As the daughter of a calligrapher, her posture is poor and her handwriting resembles chicken scratch.
