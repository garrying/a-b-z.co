St Marie φ Walker are artist-researchers who focus on the psychological dimensions of familiar objects, shared environments, and social relations. Denise St Marie attained a BFA from the [University of Victoria](https://www.uvic.ca){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} and Timothy Walker received a BA in Philosophy from the [University of Toronto](https://www.utoronto.ca/){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}. In 2017 St Marie φ Walker both achieved an MFA from the [University of Waterloo](https://uwaterloo.ca){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}, and in 2018 they were jointly awarded Gold for the [Governor General’s Academic Medal](https://www.gg.ca/document.aspx?id=15008){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}.
