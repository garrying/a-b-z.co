Lauren Wickware likes books. A lot. As a designer, she creates art books whose typography is inextricably linked to the texts and images she juggles between pages, folds and foil stamps. A graduate of [Parsons The New School](http://www.newschool.edu/parsons/){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}, she works with cultural movers and shakers like the [Art Gallery of Hamilton](http://www.artgalleryofhamilton.com/){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} and the [Aga Khan Museum](https://www.agakhanmuseum.org/){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}. For [Art Gallery of Ontario](https://www.ago.net/){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}, she recently designed the catalogue for Outsiders, a provocative exhibition of American photography from the 20th century.
