Sean Yendrys is an independent designer who works in the cultural field, often collaborating with artists, architects, curators and institutions on a wide range of work. He designed the covers for a two-book set of texts exploring infrastructure for the [Princeton Architectural Press](http://www.papress.com/html/our.home.page.tpl), and the signage for the Álvaro Siza exhibition for the [Canadian Centre for Architecture](http://www.cca.qc.ca/en/). The recipient of a MFA from [Yale University School of Art](http://art.yale.edu/) in 2014, he also teaches typography at [Parsons The New School](http://www.newschool.edu/parsons/).
