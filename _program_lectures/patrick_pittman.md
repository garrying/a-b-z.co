Patrick is a magazine obsessive caught between print and digital who has spent his career prodding, pushing and telling stories across platforms. He is the co-editor of [The Alpine Review](http://thealpinereview.com/), and editorial and creative director of [Totem](http://www.totem.tc/en/) in Toronto. He has worked as a correspondent for [Monocle](https://monocle.com/) and was the editor of the boundary-defying Australian independent [Dumbo Feather](http://www.dumbofeather.com/) magazine.
