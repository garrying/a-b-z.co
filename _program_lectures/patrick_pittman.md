Patrick is a magazine obsessive caught between print and digital who has spent his career prodding, pushing and telling stories across platforms. He is the co-editor of *[The Alpine Review](http://thealpinereview.com/){:target="_blank"}*, and editorial and creative director of [Totem](http://www.totem.tc/en/){:target="_blank"} in Toronto. He has worked as a correspondent for *[Monocle](https://monocle.com/){:target="_blank"}* and was the editor of the boundary-defying Australian independent *[Dumbo Feather](http://www.dumbofeather.com/){:target="_blank"}* magazine.
