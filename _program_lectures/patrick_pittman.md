Patrick is a magazine obsessive caught between print and digital who has spent his career prodding, pushing and telling stories across platforms. He is the co-editor of *[The Alpine Review](http://thealpinereview.com/){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}*, and editorial and creative director of [Totem](https://totemcontent.com){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} in Toronto. He has worked as a correspondent for *[Monocle](https://monocle.com/){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}* and was the editor of the boundary-defying Australian independent *[Dumbo Feather](http://www.dumbofeather.com/){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}* magazine.
