The Department of Unusual Certainties is a design studio driven by research, public engagement, and dialogue. DoUC is anti-disciplinary—blending urban design, public art, education, cartography, and social engagement. DoUC has worked with [Art Gallery of Ontario](http://ago.ca){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}, [Dansk Arkitektur Centre](https://dac.dk){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}, [Textile Museum of Canada](http://www.textilemuseum.ca){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}, [Harbourfront Centre](http://www.harbourfrontcentre.com){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}, and [Venice Biennale](http://www.labiennale.org/en/architecture/2018){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}.
