Ivona Kučerová is an Associate Professor in the [Department of Linguistics and Languages](http://linguistics.humanities.mcmaster.ca/){:target="_blank"} and the Director of the [Syntax Lab](http://www.humanities.mcmaster.ca/~kucerov/syntax_lab.html){:target="_blank"} at McMaster University in Hamilton. Her work focuses on how we systematically understand combinations of words we never heard before, how we track new versus familiar information in a conversation, and whether understanding language in a real time is computationally restricted. A native Czech speaker, her research spans Slavic, Germanic, Semitic, Inuit, and the Romance languages.
