Mindy Seu is a designer and M.Des candidate at [Harvard GSD](http://www.gsd.harvard.edu){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}. She is an [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/index.php){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} fellow for the Decentralized Web Summit. In the fall, she will begin a fellowship at the [Berkman Center](https://cyber.harvard.edu/){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} for the Internet. Previously, she was a designer at [2x4](http://2x4.org){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} and educator at [California College of the Arts](http://spring2017.designforthe.net/){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}.
