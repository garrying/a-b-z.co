Zach is an artist and educator in New York City. He worked on the [iQ font](http://vimeo.com/5233789) where a stunt driver drives a typeface, visuals for the facade of the new [Ars Electronica](https://www.aec.at/center/en/) museum, and [EyeWriter](http://www.eyewriter.org), an open source eye tracker to help a paralyzed graffiti artist draw again. Zach is a co-founder of [openFrameworks](http://openframeworks.cc) and the [School for Poetic Computation](http://sfpc.io). He teaches at [Parson School of Design](http://www.newschool.edu/parsons/).
