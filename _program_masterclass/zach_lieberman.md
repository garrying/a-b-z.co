Zach is an artist and educator in New York City. Known for projects including [_iQ font_](http://vimeo.com/5233789), [_EyeWriter_](http://www.eyewriter.org), and [_Land Lines_](https://lines.chromeexperiments.com/), his work turns type and gesture upside-down and creates new modes of expression. He is deeply committed to knowledge-sharing and education, as evidenced by his co-founding of the C++ creative coding toolkit [openFrameworks](http://openframeworks.cc) and the [School for Poetic Computation](http://www.newschool.edu/parsons/).
