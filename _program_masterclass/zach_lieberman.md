Zach is an artist and educator in New York City. Known for projects including [_iQ font_](http://vimeo.com/5233789){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}, _EyeWriter_, and [_Land Lines_](https://lines.chromeexperiments.com/){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}, his work turns type and gesture upside-down and creates new modes of expression. He is deeply committed to knowledge-sharing and education, as evidenced by his co-founding of the C++ creative coding toolkit [openFrameworks](http://openframeworks.cc){:target='_blank' rel='noopener' data-proofer-ignore=''} and the [School for Poetic Computation](http://sfpc.io){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}.
