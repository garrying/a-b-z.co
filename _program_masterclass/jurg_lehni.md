Jürg Lehni works with the nuances of technology, tools, and the human condition. His works often emerges as platforms for production and research, such as drawing machines like [Rita](http://juerglehni.com/works/rita){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} and [Hektor](http://juerglehni.com/works/hektor){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}, as well as software frameworks like [Paperjs.org](http://paperjs.org){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} and [Scriptographer.org](https://scriptographer.org){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}. In 2015, [Viktor](http://juerglehni.com/works/viktor){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'} was acquired by the [SFMOMA](https://www.sfmoma.org/artwork/2015.7){:target='_blank' rel='noopener'}. In addition to exhibiting, working and teaching internationally, Lehni runs a studio practice in Zürich.
