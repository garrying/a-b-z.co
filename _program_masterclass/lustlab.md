LUSTlab researches, generates hypotheses and makes unstable media stable again. The R&D wing of Jeroen Barendse, Thomas Castro, and Dimitri Nieuwenhuizen’s multidisciplinary design studio [LUST](http://lust.nl/){:target='_blank'}, LUSTlab brings the internet down to earth and searches for the missing link(s) between the digital and the physical. They have created [a shifting textscape at Amsterdam’s Stedelijk Museum](https://vimeo.com/82008459){:target="_blank"}, transformed weather data into [poetry at Forum in The Hague](http://lustlab.net/#beaufort-s-poetry){:target="_blank"}, and made a viewing station in which users could [‘hyperlocate’ new and abstract views of urban space](http://lustlab.net/#hyperlocator){:target="_blank"}.
