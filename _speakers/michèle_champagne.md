Michèle is a designer and writer in Toronto. She works independently with cultural institutions, publishers and broadcasters on projects for identity, publication, and public engagement.   
She designs for letters, comments, tweets, fate and debate—from crowdsourcing comments for print magazines, to building social networks with open source code. She nurtures ‘two-way-ness’ and a plurality of voices in communication.
Her practice is rooted in the Dutch conceptual design tradition, tailoring each project according to a concept, context and conversation, rather than a particular form or style.
Michèle recently spent three years in Holland with Droog, Metahaven, Mediamatic Foundation, Strelka Institute, and VPRO, a Dutch public broadcaster. In 2011, she graduated with a Master of design from Sandberg Instituut, a post-gradudate programme at Gerrit Rietveld Academie.
She mixes commissions with teaching, writing, workshops, and self-initiated research.
