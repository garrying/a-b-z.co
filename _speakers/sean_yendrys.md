Sean Yendrys is a Canadian graphic designer based between New York City and Montreal. He works independently in the cultural field, collaborating with artists, curators, architects, institutions and non-profit organizations on projects ranging from publications and websites, to identities and exhibitions. Prior to starting his own practice, he worked for the studio NODE Berlin Oslo, and with independent designers, including Geoff Han, and Neil Donnelly. He has led workshops, been a guest critic, and given lectures at schools including Concordia University, PennDesign, Rutgers University, and BHSAD Moscow. Currently he teaches both Core and Advanced Typography at Parsons The New School. He holds a BFA from Concordia University, and in 2014 received his MFA from Yale University School of Art.
